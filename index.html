Welcome to Mullaghduff

Located in the Nothwest of Donegal,Mullaghduff is a peaceful coastal community in The Rosses, West Donegal. Surrounded by beaches, lakes and islands, its the perfect base for exploring the Wild Atlantic Way. Visitors come for the scenery, the culture, and the warm Gaeltacht welcome.

Location

Mullaghduff is located in the Rosses a region in the northwest Donegal and the Wild Atlantic Way. Mullaghduff is known for its faboulous beaches, Worlds most scenic airport and worldclass hospitility.Also situated in the Gaelteacht area, the main language would be English however a lot of Irish is spoken in the area.Situated between two big towns(Dungloe and Gweedore) Mullaghduff is a small village with a population of 200 people. The village is surrounded by the Atlantic Ocean, Loughanure Lake and the Bluestack Mountains. The village is also home to a number of small businesses, including a post office, a shop, a pub and a restaurant.
