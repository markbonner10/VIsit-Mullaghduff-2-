Top Attractions

There are many attractions in MUllaghudff and the surrounding area. Some of the top attractions include:

  1. Carrickfinn Beach
  2. Donegal Airport
  3. Bonners Bar
  4. Crolly Distillery
  5. Caislean Oír
  6. Mullaghdearg Signal Tower

Bonners Bar

Located a 5 minute drive from Carrickfinn Airport.Bonners Bar is a must visit, With its 4.7 rating on tripadvisor.Bonners Bar has a wide selection of drinks and Food.Known for its Soup and toasties,Old traditional Fire and Friednly staff and efficent service.Having links to local hero Packie Bonner, there are several memorabila items from the spade used to turn the first sod in celtic Park to club pennants used in Uefa Champions league games.Bonners Bar is a must for people interested in Celtic and also history.

Crolly Distillery

Crolly Distillery is a family-owned distillery that produces high-quality Irish whiskey. The distillery offers guided tours and tastings, allowing visitors to learn about the whiskey-making process and sample the products.Orignally a factory for one of the most iconic Irish dolls in Ireland the factory was built for Crolly Dolls, which today are all over the world.Situated about 10 minutes from Mullaghduff,Crolly Distillery is a great place to learn about Irish whiskey production.

Caislean Oír

Caislean Oír is a castle located in the heart of the Gaeltacht area of Donegal.Orignally a small Irish Pub no bigger than a Garage,It has been converted into one of the most successful hotels in the region.Caislean oir is a family owned hotel situated about 7 minutes from Carrickfinn Aiport.It offers great value, With a rating of 4.8 stars its no wonder it's so popular.
